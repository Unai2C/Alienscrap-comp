# H2-02 · Live rivalry pressure

- **IF/THEN:** IF players can see live rivals, scores and MVP progress during a round, THEN at least 2 of 3 test players will say another player's score changed how they played.
- **Source section:** §5 Social by Design — repeatable social loop
- **Cheapest killing test:** desktop Explorer, two-player owner test, 10 min
- **Key metric:** number of players who change placement, artifact timing or round continuation because of a visible rival; failure if fewer than 2 of 3 do.
- **Mobile-sensitive:** yes
